# Research findings — 2026-11-09

## Celebration Coordinator
The useful job is coordinating dates, budget, guests, accepted help, food and cleanup as circumstances change. A minimum-viable celebration should be part of the initial plan, not a consolation prize. The first edition is single-organizer and local-first, with no assumed religion, family structure, gifts or obligation to host. Meal safety and accommodation details require actual verification. Multi-guest privacy needs real access control, not different Markdown files.

## Travel Copilot
Existing products already cover maps, reservations and alerts. The useful wedge is constraint-first, evidence-labelled decision support: room-specific access, total travel burden, real costs, less overpacked pacing and useful fallback choices. No autonomous booking or claim of live inventory/entry clearance. Missing official sources remain unresolved. Lower-capacity options are part of the core packet.

## Good Company Coordinator
The actual Good Company project serves nonprofit/community organizers; it is not a social companion. Its cited-answer and task-planning behavior ports well. Its deterministic ledger, retrieval engine, provider identity, claims/receipts and unattended cycles require executable integration. This template deliberately extracts the planning layer and supplies a bounded offline screening helper rather than relabelling the hosted application.

## Shared design decisions
1. Ask only the next material questions; maintain the records for the user.
2. Keep proposed vs accepted vs completed distinct. No inferred consent.
3. Work without connected accounts. Describe optional connections honestly.
4. Include procedures, blank records, fictional examples and deterministic helpers, not just a persona prompt.
5. Use ordinary, warm language; do not perform cheerfulness or fabricate personal experience.
6. Test calculations and structural privacy checks; separately acknowledge unrun conversation/security tests.

Each package has its own SOURCES.md with public source receipts. This is a product hypothesis and practical implementation, not a market-size or savings claim.
