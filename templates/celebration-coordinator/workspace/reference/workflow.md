# Workflow

1. Establish occasion, date/window and timezone, organizer role, budget/currency and what matters. Ask only missing blockers. Keep inferred observance dates tentative until confirmed from a relevant source/user.
2. Use templates/record.json when a maintained event record helps; do not make it conversational homework. Produce the requested plan. Offer alternatives when requested or a real constraint requires them. A “no event” or solo observance is valid.
3. Track invitation and RSVP states separately. Helpers are proposed until they accept. Use a headcount range or an explicitly approved planning count, not invented RSVPs.
4. Work backward: stable task ID, dependencies, due date/timezone, priority, effort, accepted owner, fallback. Include access route/seating/toilets/sensory needs, ingredient/label and cross-contact verifier, equipment, food storage, cleanup. Never certify allergy safety from a recipe. Use current authoritative food-safety guidance.
5. Forecast spending using reference/data-contract.md. Resolve duplicate costs and incompatible units before consolidating a shopping list. Unknown costs stay visible.
6. On change, show deltas, revised cost and dropped options. Don't silently change purchases, contacts, or commitments. Print a private status brief; only approved public_summary fields enter a share draft.
7. Close with receipt reconciliation, leftover/cleanup ownership and retention review. No reminders exist unless separately authorized, created and verified.

For request-sized services, practical worked cases and change handling, follow [event-studio.md](event-studio.md). Use the appropriate service rather than forcing every step for every question.
