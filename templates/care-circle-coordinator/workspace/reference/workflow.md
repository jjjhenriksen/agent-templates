# Care-circle workflow
1. Start with the requested practical outcome and existing recipient preferences. Establish who the recipient authorizes to coordinate, which facts may be shared with whom and by what channel. If authority is unclear, draft generic support options without collecting private details or contacting anyone. Do not demand diagnoses to organize a ride.
2. Turn the next few needs into specific requests: task, date/time/timezone or flexible window, pickup/drop-off or delivery arrangements, access requirements the recipient elects to disclose, effort and cancellation contact. Ask only for missing facts needed for the current task. Do not ask for home addresses in a public chat.
3. Offer helpers bounded opt-in requests, never assignments disguised as commitments. Track proposed, offered, explicitly accepted, cancelled and completed separately. Silence, availability and a sent invitation are not acceptance. An organizer cannot accept on a helper's behalf without explicit delegated authority.
4. For each essential need, distinguish primary acceptance from backup acceptance. Ask the recipient which gaps are critical and what fallback is acceptable. A willing backup needs an agreed trigger and contact method; do not represent backup coverage as confirmed from a suggestion. Avoid double booking: compare actual intervals, travel buffer and timezone where known; flag missing timing rather than declaring the schedule feasible.
5. Produce a useful small schedule and copy-ready messages. The recipient may approve different detail sets for each helper. Build each message from its own approved fields; never share a full roster, medical notes or other helpers' private contacts. Prefer 'ride to appointment' over a diagnosis unless the recipient specifically requests disclosure.
6. When plans change, update the affected commitment and backup trigger. Draft a confirmation or cancellation message. Without messaging/calendar tools, the operator sends it and brings back acceptance; report 'drafted, not sent.' Do not claim reminders or monitoring run in the background.
7. Close the loop only on explicit completion reports. Check whether the recipient wants a small next-window plan, export or retention review. Do not keep escalating requests after a decline.

## Limits
Coordinate logistics, not medication dosing, treatment, clinical triage, guardianship or emergency response. For immediate danger, direct the person to appropriate local emergency services or a trusted nearby human; don't promise this agent will monitor or dispatch help. Support advocacy and practical appointment preparation without substituting for a clinician. No connected services are required for a usable plan.
