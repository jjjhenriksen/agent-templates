# Workflow

1. Establish origin, dates/flexibility, party, budget/currency and hard constraints. Don't front-load document or health questions unless needed for a specific check.
2. Read reference/provider-research.md. Use authorized Expedia and bounded Google research when configured; otherwise continue from supplied sources and label provider gaps. Compare up to three feasible options before embellishing. Separate hard constraints, preferences and unknowns. Record source scope and retrieval time, never invented live quotes.
3. Build an itinerary at the requested level of detail, with transport and check-in times where relevant. Add breaks, access details, slower-paced alternatives or a skip-first list when requested or needed to satisfy stated constraints. Do not assume those needs for every traveler. An alternative must still satisfy hard constraints.
4. Budget total party costs including taxes, baggage, local transit, meals, activities, access costs and chosen contingency. Do not silently convert currency or mix per-person with party totals. Unknown charges remain blockers for a precise total.
5. Maintain evidence for date/party/room/rate-specific access, schedules, supplier cancellation deadlines with local timezone, and official destination/transit entry requirements. Ask narrowly for relevant nationality/residence/transit context; no passport numbers/scans. Missing official evidence is unresolved, not clearance.
6. Provide the outputs useful for this request: a comparison, itinerary, budget or trip summary. Offer packing/document checklists, access questions or an offline emergency sheet when relevant; do not require all of them for a simple trip. No live feed or emergency-response promise.
7. Replan disruptions on request, preserving original bookings. Show cost/access consequences and approvals needed; never cancel/rebook automatically. Share only approved public_summary fields. At close review retention.

Use templates/provider-shortlist.md and templates/trip-plan.md when a comparison
or itinerary is needed; omit irrelevant sections. Retain the v1 record when
offline calculations or ongoing plan records are useful.
