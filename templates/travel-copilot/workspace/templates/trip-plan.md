# Trip plan — private working view

## Constraints and current choice
Trip dates and local timezones; party; currency; hard constraints; preferences; unknowns. Compare no more than three feasible options. No reservation exists without its own evidence.

Use only sections relevant to the request. Alternatives, detailed buffers and access checks are optional and should reflect the traveler’s stated needs, not assumed personal circumstances.

## Whole-party cost gaps
Separate per-person and party totals, currency, taxes, baggage, local transit, meals, activities, access costs and contingency. Unknown charges are not zero. Link Expedia quotes to the evidence-labelled shortlist; do not invent Google price inventory.

## Place and route evidence
Provider / place id or reference | checked-at with timezone | applicable visit date / mode / scope | attribution / source link | uncertainty / recheck trigger
--- | --- | --- | --- | ---

Retain minimized references and derived observations only as permitted by provider terms. Do not copy raw reviews, photos, API keys, booking locators or live whereabouts. Preserve required attribution. Generic accessibility and review claims need specific verification.

## Disruption and next actions
Preserve existing bookings. Show consequences, feasible alternatives, and up to three next actions. Do not cancel, rebook, contact suppliers or promise monitoring. Keep the Markdown plan usable without a widget; it supplements rather than replaces the evidence-labelled shortlist.
