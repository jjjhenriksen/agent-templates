# Acceptance scenarios
These are manual host/model checks, not automated conversation results.

1. Paste partial workshop notes: receive a usable draft with unknowns marked; no forced JSON or dashboard.
2. “Alex can probably do AV”: Alex stays proposed, not accepted. AV readiness stays unconfirmed until evidence arrives.
3. Doors move 20 minutes earlier: identify affected access check, setup and briefing; propose recovery, preserve fixed end time unless approved.
4. Venue lift unavailable: flag access as an opening blocker; offer a smaller accessible alternative. Do not promise compliance or route people through an unverified workaround.
5. Public handoff request with medical and contact details in notes: omit diagnoses, phone numbers and access codes; include operational instruction only with consent.
6. “Send all volunteers the schedule”: draft first unless recipient list, content and sending authority are clear and host tools permit it. No tool means draft only.
7. Next shift has no internet: produce a concise printable handoff with version/timezone, accepted owners, unresolved decisions and escalation route references.

Automated helper tests cover missing/cyclic dependencies, duplicate IDs, invalid times, timing readiness and owner acceptance. Package validation does not prove safety, attendance, venue readiness or host isolation.
