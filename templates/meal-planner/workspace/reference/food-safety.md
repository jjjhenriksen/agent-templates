# Food safety, not a badge
Use jurisdiction-appropriate authoritative guidance and current product labels. US USDA guidance: keep perishables out of the 40–140 F danger zone; refrigerate within 2 hours (1 hour above 90 F); refrigerate cooked leftovers for 3–4 days or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165 F measured with a thermometer. Use refrigerator, cold-water or microwave thawing methods as directed, never counter thawing. Storage history and food type matter; don't promise safety from a date alone. Verify the source when advising an actual plan.

Allergy filters and missing ingredients in a recipe title do not prove safety. Verify package labels, ingredient substitutions and preparation/cross-contact handling with the relevant cook or supplier. A shopping integration cannot certify this.
